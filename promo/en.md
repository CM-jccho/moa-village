## Threads — version B (why I built it)

Ten quiet minutes every night: place one ornament, watch the dog trot past the lamp post. That's the whole app. 🐶

- 4 collectible villages: Snowbell · Sunpetal · Mochi Harbor · Lumen Alley
- 5 weather moods: clear, rain, snow, sunset, night (auto by your local time)
- Pets & rides: bicycle, scooter, balloon, tram + Mong, Nabi, Mochi — tap them and they talk
- Invite friends for coins; 5 people unlock a 40% group discount
- Rare ornaments can be sent as a gift link (`?gift=`) and land in a friend's village

No install, no login. It's a prototype, so everything is free — and `MOA-TRIAL-2026` opens a limited ornament.

👉 https://cm-jccho.github.io/moa-village/
